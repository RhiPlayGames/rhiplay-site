Delete Account

First Guess players can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal gameplay data. This applies to the account used across supported devices and the First Guess website.

How to request deletion

  1. Open First Guess and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the account settings and select Delete Account.
  3. Review the information shown, select Delete My Account, and enter DELETE MY ACCOUNT when asked to confirm.
  4. If requested, verify your identity with the sign-in provider for that account. You should not need to manually sign out first.

When the deletion service accepts your request, it will begin processing. Acceptance is not confirmation that deletion has finished, and completion may take time.

What happens to your data?

The deletion process is intended to remove your account, profile, saved progress, XP, achievements and personal leaderboard records. League membership will be removed; league ownership may be transferred or an empty league removed. The request affects the same account across your devices, including web play.

Deletion is permanent. Certain information may be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention or other legitimate obligations.

Need help?

If you cannot access the account or the in-app process does not work, email developer@rhiplaygames.com or visit our Support page. Please do not send passwords or authentication codes.
