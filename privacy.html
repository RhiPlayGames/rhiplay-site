Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RhiPlay handles information when you use First Guess through the app or online at play.rhiplaygames.com, and when you visit the RhiPlay website.

Information we collect

When you play First Guess, we may process an account identifier, your chosen player name and avatar, sign-in details supplied by your authentication provider, game progress, scores, XP, achievements and league or leaderboard activity. Guest play and signed-in play may use different identifiers.

Technical information, such as device and browser details, IP addresses, diagnostics and access logs, may also be processed to operate and protect the service. Support requests may include the information you choose to send us.

How we use information

We use this information to provide gameplay, save and synchronise progress, manage accounts and leagues, display rankings, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, investigate technical issues and maintain the security and reliability of First Guess.

Third-party services

First Guess uses services provided by Google Firebase, including authentication, cloud data storage and server-side functionality. Authentication may also involve Google or Apple when you choose those sign-in methods. Website hosting, platform providers and other infrastructure services may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the service.

Those services have their own applicable privacy information. We will update this policy if the services or information we process change materially.

Data retention

Account and gameplay information is kept while needed to operate First Guess. If you request account deletion, we aim to remove the account and associated personal gameplay data as part of the deletion process, subject to information that we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention or other legitimate obligations. Requests may take time to complete.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Signed-in players can request account deletion from First Guess under the account settings. See our Delete Account page for instructions, or contact support if you need assistance.

Children

We do not intend to collect personal information from children in circumstances requiring parental consent without appropriate safeguards. If you have a concern about a child's data, please contact support.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as First Guess evolves. The latest version and revision date will be published here.
