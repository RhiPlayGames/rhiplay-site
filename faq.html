PLAYER HELP

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play First Guess in my browser?

Yes. Open play.rhiplaygames.com in a compatible browser. You do not need to download the Android or iOS app to try the web version.

What happens if I answer incorrectly in Classic?

An incorrect guess uses one of your three lives. You can choose Next Clue without using a life, so you do not have to guess at every clue.

Does using another clue cost a life?

No. In Classic, it lowers the XP available for a correct answer but does not cost a life.

How do I get the First Guess bonus?

Answer correctly on clue one in Classic to earn 100 bonus XP on top of the normal clue-one score.

What is Daily Flash 5?

It is a daily set of five challenges, offering double XP.

Can I keep progress across devices?

Supported sign-in and account features can synchronise certain progress across devices. Guest progress may not always be recoverable if the browser is reset or you change devices.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the instructions on the Delete Account page. You can also contact support if you have trouble accessing your account.

Where can I report a problem?

Use the Support page. Include your device, browser or operating system and a description of what happened, where possible.

Where do I find the full rules?

Read our How to Play guide and Game Modes page.
