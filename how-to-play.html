FIRST GUESS GUIDE

How to Play First Guess

First Guess rewards both knowledge and judgement. You may know the answer immediately, or you may need more clues. The challenge is choosing when to guess.

Classic: the basics

  1. Start a Classic challenge and read clue one.
  2. Enter your answer if you think you know it, or use Next Clue to see more information.
  3. You have three lives for the challenge. An incorrect guess costs a life; choosing Next Clue does not.
  4. There are up to ten clues. Answer before your lives run out, or choose Give Up when you are ready to stop.

How Classic XP works

A correct answer on clue one earns 100 XP, then the available score falls by 10 XP with each later clue. Clue ten awards 10 XP. Getting the answer right on the very first clue earns an additional 50 XP bonus, for up to 150 XP.

Choosing when to guess

If the first clue is too broad, use Next Clue instead of making a risky guess. Later clues become more specific, but they are worth fewer points. In other game modes, the available clues, guesses, lives and scoring can be different.

Daily and themed play

Daily Flash 5 offers five daily challenges with double XP. Case Files lets you progress through themed adventures and missions in sequence. Compare the game modes for more details.
